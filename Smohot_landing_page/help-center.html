Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is the purpose of this platform?

    This platform is designed to provide a social networking experience where users can connect with friends, family, and communities, engage in discussions, and participate in buying and selling activities.

  • Is this platform safe?

    We prioritize user safety and have implemented measures to protect your personal information and transactions. However, it's essential to exercise caution when dealing with strangers online.

  • Can I block a user?

    Yes, you can block users who are sending unwanted or harassing messages.

  • How do I report a user for inappropriate behavior?

    There is a reporting feature within the messaging interface. Please use it to report any violations of our terms of service.

  • How do I send a message to another user?

    Simply find the user's profile, click on their name, and start typing your message.

  • How do I list an item for sale?

    In the selling section, there is a hovering 'Sell' button. Click it, and you'll need to provide details about the item, including photos, price, and description.

  • How do I make a purchase?

    Once you find an item you're interested in, contact the seller directly to negotiate terms and arrange payment.

  • How do I create an account?

    You can create an account using your phone number or email address.

  • Are messages encrypted for privacy?

    We employ strong encryption measures to protect your conversations.

    Once you find an item you're interested in, contact the seller directly to negotiate terms and arrange payment.

  • How do I update my profile information?

    Visit your profile settings to edit your personal details, photos, and preferences.

  • How do I reset my password?

    If you forget your password, you can request a password reset link.

  • What should I do if I'm having trouble logging in?

    Check your internet connection and ensure you're entering the correct credentials. If the issue persists, contact our support team.

  • My messages aren't loading. What can I do?

    Try refreshing the page or restarting your device. If the problem continues, let us know.

  • What types of statuses can I share?

    You can share text updates, photos, videos, and links to external content.

  • Can I edit or delete my listings after publishing?

    Yes, you can modify or remove your listings within a certain timeframe.

  • What devices are compatible with video and voice calls on this platform?

    Our platform supports video and voice calls on most modern smartphones, tablets, and computers with a camera and microphone.

  • Are there any requirements for a stable connection?

    A strong internet connection is recommended for the best quality video and voice calls.
